FI COMMAND CENTER V14.0.81

Hybrid Presentation uses the Live Operations brushed-silver frames and
black text on silver panels. The selected mini tool card fills most of
the carousel, with the tool photo beside its details and translucent
previews of the immediate neighbors. The summary and navigation remain
visible above and below the carousel.
